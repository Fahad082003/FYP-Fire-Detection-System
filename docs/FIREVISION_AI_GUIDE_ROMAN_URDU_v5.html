1. FireVision AI Kya Hai?

Note (2026-10-26): is tareekh se project Django par chalta hai (sentinel-django/): ek hi server (port 5291) dashboard ke pages, REST API aur live updates sab deta hai. Purana ASP.NET Core API (backend/) aur Angular dashboard (frontend/) delete ho chuke hain, sirf git history mein hain. Zyada tar tasveerein (2026-10-28 se) naye Django dashboard ki hain, jo test database, asli AI models aur test video ke saath li gayin. Yeh kuch abhi purane Angular dashboard ki hain: evacuation-before.png, evacuation-camera-people.png, camera-safe-danger-zones.png (aur swagger.png, jo ab maujood hi nahi).

Ek line mein: FireVision AI ek fire-safety control room software hai. Ye building ke cameras (CCTV aur drone), dhuwen/garmi ke sensors aur smart sprinklers ko ek screen par jorta hai. AI camera mein aag, dhuwan aur insaan pehchaanta hai. Khatra ho to system khud:

  • screen par alarm aur alert dikhata hai,
  • SMS, Email aur browser Push notification bhejta hai,
  • us zone ke sprinklers chala sakta hai,
  • har waqia ka log rakhta hai,
  • building ke naqshe par mehfooz raasta aur bahar nikalne ka andazan waqt batata hai.

Asal zindagi ki misaal

Ek factory ka security room sochiye: deewar par TV screens, aur ek guard jo sab dekh raha hai. FireVision AI us guard ka computer version hai:

3. Poora Flow: Camera Se Screen Tak

Misaal: warehouse ke camera mein aag lagi

1. Camera frame          → AI Service (sentinel_ai) har second 1–4 frame leta hai
2. AI Service            → person + fire/smoke model chalata hai, boxes nikalta hai
3. Tracking              → har cheez ko track id (F11, S2, P3)
4. Confirmation          → wohi aag 3 frames mein dikhi? tab hi report
5. Severity              → aag ke boxes frame ka kitna % → Low/Medium/High/Critical
6. AI danger zone        → aag ke gird 25% bara laal zone
7. POST /api/detections  → Django server (X-Device-Key ke saath)
8. validation.validate   → JSON ki jaanch + insaan danger zone mein? (server khud hisaab)
9. events.publish        → SSE "detection" → sab dashboards par boxes
10. alerts.evaluate_detection → incident banao/barhao (dedupe ke saath)
11. IncidentLogs         → Opened, FireMeasurement, PersonInDanger
12. Emergency?           → SMS/Email/Push queue + Auto sprinklers (simulated data par nahi)
13. SSE /api/events      → "incident", "incidentLog", "activity" → dashboard update
14. Evacuation panel     → us zone ka danger zone active → naya mehfooz raasta
15. Calibrated camera    → camera mein dikha insaan naqshe par amber nuqta (Chapter 9)
16. Risk prediction      → aag kin kamron tak pahunch sakti hai + naqshe par risk heatmap (Chapter 9)

Bina refresh ke kaise? Browser aur server ke darmiyan Server-Sent Events (SSE) connection GET /api/events hamesha khula rehta hai (shell.js mein EventSource). Pehle poora snapshot aata hai, phir har naya event. Connection toote to browser khud dobara jurta hai. Canvas har animation frame (~60 dafa per second) par dobara draw hota hai.

Detection ab sirf ek raaste se aati hai:

  • REST: POST /api/detections (AI service aur simulator dono yehi use karte hain).
  • Purana SignalR hub method PublishDetection (.NET version) Django mein nahi hai; SSE sirf server → browser jata hai.

9. Floor Plans, Evacuation Raasta Aur Waqt

Naqsha banana (Floor Plans page)

Tool Kaam
+ Room / + Corridor Chalne ki jagah
+ Wall Deewar (0.2 m moti)
+ Door Darwaza (deewar par). "Blocked" = band
+ Exit Emergency exit
+ Safe zone / + Danger zone Mehfooz / khatarnak ilaqa. Danger zone ko camera zone (jaise Ground Floor - Warehouse) se jor sakte hain
  • Apne naqshe ki PNG/JPG background mein upload kar sakte hain (5 MB tak, file ke andar ke bytes check hote hain).
  • Tasveer sirf background hai. System tasveer se deewarein khud nahi pehchaanta.
  • Har cheez meters mein, 0.1 m par snap. Backend 2,000 elements tak, ghalti par 400 aur saaf message.

Raasta kaise nikalta hai (geometry.find_route)

  1. Naqsha 0.5 m ke khanon (grid) mein. Bari building par khana bara (max ~200,000 khane).
  2. Chal sakte hain: khule Room/Corridor/Door/Exit/SafeZone. Band: deewar, band darwaza, active danger zone.
  3. Danger zone active hai jab "Active now" tick ho, ya us ke linked zone mein khula incident ho.
  4. Dijkstra (8 simtein): qareeb tareen pohanch-sakne-wala khula exit.
  5. 1 m clearance: danger zone se 1 m ke andar wale khane mehngay. Koi raasta 1 m door na reh sake to saaf warning: "CLEARANCE VIOLATED".
  6. Insaan khud danger zone ke andar ho to bahar nikalne ki ijazat, message "leave immediately".

Waqt (ETA)

Andazan waqt = faasla (m) ÷ 1.2 m/s       misaal: 26.9 m ÷ 1.2 = ~22 s

Dhuwan, bheer, seedhiyan shamil nahi. Is liye screen par "estimate" likha hai.

Aag kahan phailegi (risk prediction + risk heatmap)

  1. Aag = naqshe ke active danger zones: "Active now" tick, ya linked zone (jaise Ground Floor - Warehouse) mein khula incident.
  2. Naqsha 1 m ke khanon mein. Aag har khane tak kamron, corridors aur darwazon se kitni door hai, ye nikalta hai. Deewar rokti hai. Band darwaza nahi rokta, kyunki band darwaza aag ko sirf dheema karta hai.
  3. Faasle se darja:

High ≤ 5 m Laal
Medium ≤ 10 m Narangi
Low ≤ 20 m Peela
None 20 m se zyada, ya deewar ke peeche (darwaza nahi) Koi rang nahi

Dashboard par: Evacuation panel mein naqshe ke upar heatmap, aur neeche "⚠ Fire risk: Next at risk: Main corridor (7.4 m, Medium), Hall B (8 m, Medium)". Kisi zone mein incident khule ya band ho to ye khud dobara hisaab hota hai.

Camera ko naqshe se jorna (calibration / homography)

Masla: camera tasveer ko pixels mein dekhta hai, naqsha meters mein hai. Pehle system ko pata nahi tha ke camera mein dikha insaan naqshe par kahan hai, is liye operator ko haath se click karna padta tha.

Hal: har camera ke liye 4 jodiyan: farsh ke 4 kone camera ki tasveer mein kahan hain (pixels) aur naqshe par kahan hain (meters). In se ek 3×3 hisaab (homography) banta hai jo tasveer ka har farsh wala pixel naqshe ke meter mein badal deta hai. Bilkul jaise mobile ka document scanner tirchi photo ko seedha kar deta hai.

Kaise lagayein (Floor Plans page → Camera calibration):

  1. Naqsha kholein aur pehle Save karein.
  2. Camera chunein.
  3. Har point ke liye Camera px (x, y) likhein. Camera ki ek snapshot Paint mein kholein, kone par mouse rakhein, Paint neeche x, y dikhata hai.
  4. Plan m likhein, ya Plan m ⌖ dabayein aur naqshe par wohi kona click karein.
  5. Save calibration. Hatana ho to Remove.

Is ke baad: dashboard ke Evacuation panel mein us camera ke har insaan ka amber nuqta naqshe par aata hai (insaan ke pairon ka point: box ka neeche beech). Kisi nuqte par click karein to raasta usi insaan ki jagah se nikalta hai.

Cheez Tafseel

Seedhi ya mezzanine par khade insaan ki jagah ghalat ho sakti hai. - Camera hila to calibration dobara karni hogi. - Fish-eye/wide lens mein kinare mude hote hain, wahan andaza kam sahi. - Ek insaan do cameras mein dikhe to do nuqte. - Nuqta andaza hai (kuch decimeter tak farq), GPS nahi. - Raasta khud tab nikalta hai jab nuqte par click karein; har insaan ka raasta bina click ke nahi. - Calibration save karne par sirf usi browser ki list update hoti hai; doosre khule dashboards reload ke baad dekhte hain. - Asli camera ke saath abhi test nahi hua: simulator ke nakli insaanon par check kiya.
